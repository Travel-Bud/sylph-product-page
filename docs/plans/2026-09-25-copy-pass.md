# Landing copy pass (2026-09-25)

The live page (`/`, Mock B) read as machine-written and was too long to skim. This pass cut visible text from about
1,760 words to about 990 and the page from 9,426px to 7,804px at 1440.

## Rules the copy now follows

- One headline and at most one supporting sentence per section. A skimmer reading only headlines gets the pitch:
  Stop chasing receipts. Priya just texts a photo. Dana sets the rules once. Priya hears back right away. Twenty
  charges, five reach Dana. Month end is already done.
- Say a thing once. "The rule, the threshold and the amount" and "the same charge gets the same answer" were
  repeated about ten times; each now appears once, where it answers a question (the FAQ).
- No slogan cadence: no "One X. Two Y. Nothing to Z.", no "Every X, every Y" lists, no "Nothing to A, no B to C".
- Plain words over product words: "sets the rules" not "compiles", "blocked charge stays off the total" not
  "kept off the reimbursable total".
- No narration chrome. Cut: step counters (1/5), the captions on every band, name badges on every section, the
  second tag on every card, the "In build" teaser.
- A section earns its place only if a buyer would miss it. Cut: the interactive sample month (it repeated the desk at
  a bigger scale in 440 words), three of the eight questions (answered elsewhere on the page).
