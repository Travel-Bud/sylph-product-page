# janus: Two faces

**Idea.** The page is split down the middle the whole way down: Priya's paper face on the left, Dana's ink face on the right. As you scroll, her column rises and his descends, and they line up only on each shared moment. Whatever sits on the seam (headline, clock, report) is drawn in both faces and cut at the edge, whole only while they align. The Sushi Kanda charge crosses the seam at every handover. At month end the two halves of the report meet, and a green ground opens from the seam and covers both faces.

**Built.** `/explore/janus`: a first screen with both faces, the seam and the charge; four paired beats (receipt, rule M-041, note, approval); month end; the close with price and Book a demo. On a phone the seam runs across the screen, Priya above and Dana below, and the strips slide sideways against each other. Reduced motion shows the settled rows. No gen-image spend: existing cast only.
