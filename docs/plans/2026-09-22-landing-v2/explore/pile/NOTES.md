# The pile (/explore/pile)

**Idea.** The page is a data story. The sample month (98 charges) opens as a heap of receipt slips, and scrolling sorts it: receipts fan in from text, email and upload to a September calendar; foreign charges hop from their printed amount to dollars on a log axis; duplicates are ringed and look-alikes arced across the month; the rules sweep through rows with their thresholds, each exception labelled with its citation; the 84 cleared file into a report and the 14 left are listed by name. The "left for a person" counter drops from 98 to 14 as the marks turn green. Every mark can be hovered or tapped.

**Built.** Canvas marks, HTML labels, Newsreader for the prose. Reduced motion settles each step instantly. There's no sound and no gen-image spend. A table of all 98 charges and an "About this sample" note sit under the close.

**To ship.** Drive the story from the real engine output instead of the local check. Add keyboard focus for the marks (the table covers screen readers for now) and test on real phones.

**Unfinished.** The phone text window under the sticky graphic is tight on long steps. On the duplicates step at 390, the Tim Hortons label crowds its arc.
