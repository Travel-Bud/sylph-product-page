# The pile (/explore/pile)

**Idea.** A data story: the sample month (98 charges) opens as a heap of receipt slips, and scrolling sorts it: receipts fan in from text, email and upload to a September calendar; foreign charges hop from their printed amount to dollars on a log axis; duplicates are ringed and look-alikes arced across the month; the rules sweep through rows, each exception labelled with its citation; the 84 cleared file into a report and the 14 left are listed by name. The "left for a person" counter drops from 98 to 14 as the marks turn green. Any mark can be hovered or tapped.

**Built.** Canvas marks, HTML labels, Newsreader for the prose. Reduced motion settles each step instantly. There's no sound and no gen-image spend. A table of all 98 charges sits under the close.

**To ship.** Drive the story from the real engine output instead of the local check. Add keyboard focus for the marks (the table covers screen readers for now).

**Unfinished.** The phone text panel just fits the longest step at 390x844; shorter phones may clip it. On the duplicates step at 390, the Tim Hortons label crowds its arc.
