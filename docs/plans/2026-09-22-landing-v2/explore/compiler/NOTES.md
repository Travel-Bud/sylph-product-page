# Compiled (/explore/compiler)

**Idea.** The policy is the protagonist and the page is its editor: a plain-language policy, the rules it compiles to, a sample week of charges. Scrub or type an amount, flip an outcome, strike a line or add a sentence, and every charge re-verdicts citing its line, with wires tracing line to rule to charge. The draft is diffed against the approved ruleset and fingerprinted; undo and the fingerprint returns. "Check again" gives the same answers.

**Built.** The editor, with a phone form and a sticky status dock. Four scenes: compile time versus check time, the passes before any rule runs, month-end exports, and a questionnaire that compiles into the editor. A close with price and demo. Existing objects only, $0 gen-image.

**To ship.** Align rule IDs and grammar with the real engine; test scrubbing on real touch devices.

**Brief concern.** Re-verdicting a week against a draft implies previewing a policy change against past charges. If Sylph cannot, label the week illustrative.
