# The receipt (/lab/receipt)

**Idea.** The page is the Sushi Kanda receipt. A print head is fixed to the bottom of the screen, so scrolling feeds the paper and each line develops as it comes out. The receipt tells its own month, and every event is product truth: texted, matched to its card charge, read against M-041, noted, one of 5 of 20 in Dana's queue, approved, filed on line 3, exported. Priya and Dana appear only at the edges, seen from below by the paper.

**Built.** A first screen that prints itself. Seven scenes: camera flash, card-feed match, cap meter with stamps, Priya typing her note, the pile sorting down to five, Dana's Approve (click it, or scroll and she does), export previews, a thermal fade scrubbed by scroll, then the tear. The close is a second receipt carrying the price. Reduced motion shows the finished strip. Two new renders, `pov-priya` and `pov-dana`: gpt-image-2 edits of the existing cast, about $0.52.

**To ship.** Metadata and an OG image, a voice pass with Ben, and confirmation that "nothing to install" matches SMS onboarding. Widths from 390 to 1920 wheel-checked 2026-09-23.

**Unfinished.** Only clicks make sound. Mobile peeks are small.
